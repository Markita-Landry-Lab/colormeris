How it works
  1. Open a PDF, PNG or JPG (or drop / paste it). For PDFs pick the page and resolution.
  2. Add one panel per heatmap.
  3. Grid: click the outer top-left and bottom-right corners of the heatmap, then set rows, columns and labels.
  4. Colorbar: click the two ends of the colorbar along its middle, then click at least two tick marks and type their values.
  5. Check the results (toggle Reconstruct to compare colors), then download CSV or export the project zip to reload later.
  1. Open a PDF, PNG or JPG of the IVIS figure (or drop / paste it). For PDFs pick the page and resolution.
  2. Grid (optional): if the animals are arranged in boxes, click the outer top-left and bottom-right corners of the grid, then check rows, columns and box names.
  3. Colorbar: click the two ends of the colorbar, then click at least two ticks and type their values (e.g. 0.4e8 for 0.4 × 10⁸).
  4. Regions: draw ellipses (Shift = circle), rectangles (Shift = square) or polygons. A region drawn in one box is copied into every box; drag a copy to nudge it in that box only.
  5. Grayscale pixels (the photo) count as no signal. Read the results table, download the CSV, or export the project zip.

Switch tools at the top at any time: the file and both tools' work stay, and one project zip holds everything. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, drag handles to adjust. Esc cancels, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes. Everything runs in your browser; files are never uploaded, except page images sent to OpenRouter when you run the agent.
