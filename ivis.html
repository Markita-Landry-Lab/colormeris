How it works
  1. Open a PDF, PNG or JPG of the IVIS figure (or drop / paste it). For PDFs pick the page and resolution.
  2. Grid (optional): if the animals are arranged in boxes, click the outer top-left and bottom-right corners of the grid, then check rows, columns and box names.
  3. Colorbar: click the two ends of the colorbar, then click at least two ticks and type their values (e.g. 0.4e8 for 0.4 × 10⁸).
  4. Regions: draw ellipses (Shift = circle), rectangles (Shift = square) or polygons. A region drawn in one box is copied into every box; drag a copy to nudge it in that box only.
  5. Grayscale pixels (the photo) count as no signal. Read the results table, download the CSV, or export the project zip.

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, drag handles to adjust. Esc cancels, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes. Everything runs in your browser; files are never uploaded.
